Paper Contribution Outline

Title Direction:
Quantum Variational Autoencoder Based Cross-Attention Multimodal EEG-ECG Fusion for Emotion Recognition


Main Contributions:

1. Multimodal Fusion Framework
- Develop an EEG-ECG fusion architecture combining modality-specific encoders with cross-modal attention.
- Enables adaptive interaction between physiological modalities.


2. Quantum Latent Representation Learning
- Introduce a quantum bottleneck after multimodal fusion.
- Investigate whether quantum-inspired latent transformation improves representation learning.


3. Comprehensive Ablation Study
- Compare:
  EEG-only
  ECG-only
  Simple fusion
  Attention fusion
  Classical bottleneck
  MLP bottleneck
  Cross-attention without quantum
  Proposed QVAE model


4. Robustness Evaluation
- Perform multi-seed evaluation.
- Analyze validity under:
  Correct pairing
  Same-subject wrong trial pairing
  Cross-subject pairing


5. Experimental Validation
- Demonstrate stable performance across multiple random seeds.
- Provide quantitative analysis of each architectural component.
